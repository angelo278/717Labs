Privacy policy

Last updated 27 November 2026

Who we are

717Labs helps businesses get recommended in AI search. This policy explains what personal information we collect through 717labs.co.uk, why, and your rights. The person responsible for your information (the “data controller”) is Angelo Montersino, trading as 717Labs, of Flat 31, Wellington House, Lewis Street, Cardiff CF11 6JY, United Kingdom. You can contact us about anything in this policy at info@717labs.co.uk.

What we collect

  • When you request a free AI visibility report: your name, work email address, website and any example searches you choose to give us.
  • When you book a consultation: your name, email address, the time you chose, your time zone and, if you give it, your website.
  • When you email us: your email address, name and anything you choose to include in your message.
  • When you become a client: the contact and billing details we need to provide our services and invoice you.

We do not collect sensitive information, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted providers to run our business, and they only process your information on our behalf:

  • Website hosting and forms: Netlify, which stores form submissions and consultation bookings.
  • Report preparation: GitHub, which runs the automated process that prepares your report; OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Perplexity and SerpApi, whose AI and search tools we use to test how businesses appear in AI answers; and Resend, which sends your report and booking confirmations by email.
  • Email and video calls: Google Workspace, which hosts our email and the video calls we hold with you.
  • Banking: our bank, which receives client payments, for clients only.

To prepare your report we look at your public website and test AI tools with questions about your industry. The AI tools receive your website address, text from your public website and any example searches you gave us. We do not put your name or email address into AI tools.

International transfers

Our providers and our team may process information outside the UK, including in the United States and Malawi. Where this happens, we rely on the safeguards UK law requires, such as approved contract terms.

How long we keep it

  • Report requests: up to 12 months, unless you become a client.
  • Consultation bookings: up to 12 months, unless you become a client.
  • Client records: for as long as we work together, then up to 6 years for tax and accounting purposes.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop using it for follow-up emails, or restrict how we use it
  • send it to you or another organisation in a portable format

Email info@717labs.co.uk and we will reply within one month. If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
