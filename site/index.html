The shift

The shortlist is increasingly being shaped by AI.

Customers are increasingly asking AI assistants questions such as:

“Who are the best accountants for small businesses in Manchester?” “Which financial adviser should I speak to?” “Can you recommend a reliable building company near me?”

Rather than presenting a long list of websites, AI can provide a handful of recommendations and compare the most suitable options.

Traditional search

A list of websites to explore.

Customers compare rankings, websites and options.

AI-powered search

A shortlist of businesses to consider.

Customers receive a conversational answer, often with a small number of recommendations.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is answer engine optimisation (AEO)?

Answer engine optimisation is the process of improving how your business is understood, represented and discovered across AI-powered search platforms. The objective is to make your business a credible and relevant recommendation when potential customers are looking for your services.

Is this a software subscription?

No. 717Labs is a fully managed service.

We use AI-powered tools to research, analyse and monitor your visibility, but we also carry out the improvements needed across your website and wider online presence.

You aren’t simply paying for access to a dashboard. You’re paying for the ongoing work, expertise and support that goes into improving your visibility.

Why does AI recommend my competitors rather than us?

There isn’t always one simple explanation.

AI platforms draw on information from different sources, including websites, business listings, reviews and other online publications. They use this information to understand businesses and determine which ones are relevant to a particular question.

Your competitors may have clearer website information, a stronger online presence, more relevant third-party references or a better-established reputation in the sources AI systems consult.

We investigate these differences and identify where improvements could help your business become a stronger candidate for recommendation.

Can you guarantee that ChatGPT will recommend us?

No. No agency can guarantee that an independent AI platform will recommend a particular business.

AI-generated answers can change depending on the question, location, context and platform. We don’t control those systems or their recommendations.

What we can do is improve the quality, clarity and credibility of the information available about your business, monitor how it appears and work consistently towards better visibility.

We believe in showing you the work and the evidence rather than making promises we cannot guarantee.

How long does it take to see results?

There isn’t a universal timeframe.

Some improvements, particularly clearer website information and corrected business details, can be made relatively quickly. Changes in how AI platforms discover, interpret and reference your business may take longer.

That is why our service operates on a monthly basis. We establish a starting position, implement improvements and monitor developments over time.

Our initial three-month commitment gives us an opportunity to carry out meaningful work and assess progress.

What will you need from us?

Very little of your time.

We’ll begin with a short introductory conversation to understand your business, customers and objectives.

We’ll then need access to relevant business information and, where appropriate, your website or the ability to coordinate changes with whoever manages it.

You’ll be asked to review and approve proposed changes before publication. Beyond that, we manage the ongoing research, implementation and reporting.

Which AI assistants do you cover?

Our monitoring and optimisation work can cover leading AI-powered search and answer platforms, including ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, Microsoft Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, DeepSeek and Mistral.

The platforms included in your monitoring programme will depend on your business, target audience and agreed scope of work.

Do you work with businesses outside the UK?

Yes.

Although 717Labs is based around the UK market, our approach can support businesses in other countries too.

We can assess how your business appears to customers in different markets and identify opportunities to strengthen your visibility internationally.

Can we change our retainer or stop?

Yes.

Every engagement starts with a three-month minimum commitment. Once that initial period has been completed, you can change the scope of your service or end the arrangement at the close of any subsequent month.

We want clients to continue working with us because they see the value in the service, not because they are locked into a lengthy contract.
